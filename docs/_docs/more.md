### FAQ {#faq}

**Why can't I delete a task?**

Klaar cancels tasks instead of deleting them, on purpose. Every change Klaar makes is a small rewrite of one line that you can undo, and nothing it does removes text from your notes. A cancelled task (`- [-]`) also stays in your journal as a record of what you dropped. Reopen task brings it back.

To delete a task for good, click its text to open the note at that line, then delete the line there, where Cmd-Z works as usual.
