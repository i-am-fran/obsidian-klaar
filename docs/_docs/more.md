### Support {#support}

Klaar is free. If it helps you, you can [buy me a coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/iamfran). Found a bug or something that looks off in your theme? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/i-am-fran/obsidian-klaar/issues).
